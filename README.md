# Senimatik

**The IP-first art marketplace that protects provenance, grows artists, and turns ownership into long-lasting legacy.**

**[🌐 Live Demo](https://senimatik.com/) **

---

## 🎨 The Problem

Today's art platforms fail to help both artists and buyers. Talented creators get lost in the noise. Collectors hesitate without proof. Original work gets stolen with impunity.

- **No Discovery** — Artists struggle to promote their arts
- **No Trust Layer** — Collectors want to feel the art journey & experience before purchase
- **Intellectual Properties Theft** — Original work is copied, uploaded & used for commercial without artist's consent

---

## ✨ What We're Solving

An **IP-native marketplace**, not just another art store. We flip the script by making authenticity, provenance, and artist growth the foundation—not an afterthought.

- **Curated Artist Discovery** — Help artists reach the right collectors through featured drops, better recommendations, and curated visibility inside the marketplace
- **Authenticity + Smart Licensing** — Verify each artwork with files, drafts, and metadata, then attach clear personal, commercial, or limited-use protecting rights to every sale
- **Collector Centric** — Web2-friendly experience to connect collectors to style, budget, and story instead of wallet jargon

---

## 🚀 What is Senimatik?

A **verified creator licensing marketplace** built on Solana with:

- **For Creators:** Upload your art, set flexible license types (Personal Use, Commercial, Limited Editions), earn royalties on every resale
- **For Collectors:** Buy with confidence. Get verifiable proof of license ownership on-chain. Use art commercially with automatic royalty splits
- **For Everyone:** Combat art theft with creator verification. Get clear, enforceable usage rights instead of ambiguous "right-click saves"

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- **Node.js** 18+ and npm/pnpm
- **Solana Wallet** (Phantom, Solflare, or Coinbase Wallet)
- **Convex Account** (get at [convex.dev](https://convex.dev))
- **Cloudflare R2** account for file uploads (optional for dev)

---

### First Steps
- **Create Account** — Connect wallet via Solana Wallet Adapter
- **Apply as Creator** — Fill out application form (auto-approved in dev)
- **Create Artwork** — Go through 6-step creation flow
- **Browse Discover** — See all created artworks
- **View Studio** — Dashboard with your listings
